Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Drafting Helpers ("we", "us", "our") operates the website draftinghelpers.com. This page explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our site.

Information We Collect

Information you provide: When you fill out our consultation form, we collect your name, email address, firm name (optional), the role you are interested in, and any details you share in the message field.

Information collected automatically: We use Google Analytics 4 and may use Microsoft Clarity to collect anonymized usage data such as pages visited, time on site, device type and approximate location. We do not collect financial information through the website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your staffing inquiry and provide a custom plan
  • To improve our website and services based on aggregated usage data
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising (Google Ads conversion tracking)

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies set by Google Analytics, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with service providers (e.g., Netlify for hosting, Google for analytics) who assist in operating our website, subject to confidentiality agreements.

Data Retention

We retain form submissions for as long as necessary to provide our services and follow up on your inquiry. Analytics data is retained per the default retention periods of the respective platforms.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us at the address below. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA.

Security

We use HTTPS encryption and follow industry-standard practices to protect your data. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
