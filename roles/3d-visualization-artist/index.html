Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote 3D Visualization Artist Handles for Your Firm

Exterior and interior renderings

Produces still renderings from your Revit, SketchUp or Rhino models for client presentations and design reviews.

Design option studies

Renders quick side-by-side options for massing, materials and facade studies so clients can make decisions faster.

Animations and walkthroughs

Creates flythroughs and walkthrough videos for marketing, community meetings and planning commission hearings.

Entitlement and planning visuals

Prepares photo-matched site views and context renderings for design review boards and public hearings.

Proposal and marketing imagery

Supplies images for RFQ responses, the firm website and social media, matched to your visual style.

Post-production

Finishes renders in Photoshop with entourage, landscaping, sky replacement and color correction.

Material and asset libraries

Builds and organizes reusable material, furniture and landscape libraries so each new project starts faster.
