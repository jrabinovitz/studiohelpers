Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote Administrative Assistant Handles for Your Firm

Inbox and calendar management

Triages email, schedules client meetings, site visits and consultant calls, and sends reminders.

Invoicing and AR follow-up

Prepares monthly invoices from timesheets and contracts, sends them out and follows up on overdue payments.

Timesheet and project setup

Collects staff timesheets, sets up new projects and phases in your accounting system, and flags budget overruns.

Contract and proposal admin

Formats owner-architect agreements and consultant contracts from your templates and tracks signatures.

Project file organization

Keeps project folders, transmittals and document archives organized and consistent across jobs.

Vendor and consultant coordination

Collects certificates of insurance, W-9s and consultant invoices, and keeps records current.

Licensing and CE tracking

Tracks architect license renewals, AIA continuing education hours and firm registrations so nothing lapses.
