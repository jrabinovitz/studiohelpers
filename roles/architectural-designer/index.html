Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote Architectural Designer Handles for Your Firm

Schematic design support

Develops plan options, massing studies and diagrams from your sketches and program so you can review more ideas in less time.

Space planning and test fits

Produces test fits and furniture layouts for tenant improvement, office and multifamily unit studies.

Design development drawings

Carries the design into DD with refined plans, elevations, sections and preliminary details.

Zoning and code research

Pulls zoning setbacks, FAR, height limits and occupancy data into code analysis sheets for your architect to verify.

Presentation packages

Assembles client presentation boards and slide decks with plans, diagrams and renderings.

Product and material research

Researches products, finishes and manufacturer data, and prepares cut sheets and options for review.

Production backup

Steps into CD production and redlines whenever a deadline needs extra hands.
