Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote BIM Modeler Handles for Your Firm

Model building from schematic design

Takes SD sketches, massing or CAD backgrounds and builds the Revit model with walls, floors, roofs, openings and levels set up properly.

Family creation

Builds parametric doors, windows, casework, fixtures and annotation families that follow your naming and parameter standards.

Sheets, views and schedules

Sets up sheets, view templates, legends and door, window and room schedules that update with the model.

LOD progression

Develops the model from LOD 200 through LOD 300 and 350 as the project moves from DD into CDs.

Clash detection and coordination

Links consultant models, runs clash checks in Navisworks, and logs issues for your team to resolve with MEP and structural.

Template and content management

Cleans up and maintains your office template, shared parameters and content library.

CAD-to-Revit conversion

Rebuilds legacy CAD projects and existing-conditions drawings as working Revit models.
