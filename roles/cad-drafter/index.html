Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote CAD Drafter Handles for Your Firm

Construction document sets

Drafts floor plans, elevations, sections and enlarged plans for permit and CD sets using your sheet templates and title blocks.

Redline pickup

Turns marked-up PDFs and hand sketches into clean, revised sheets, and keeps revision clouds, deltas and issue logs current.

Detail library work

Builds and maintains standard wall, roof, window and waterproofing details so your team stops redrawing the same conditions on every job.

Door, window and finish schedules

Builds and updates schedules and keeps tags coordinated with the plans as the design changes.

As-builts and existing conditions

Converts field measurements, scans and old PDFs into accurate existing-conditions drawings for renovation and TI projects.

Xref and layer cleanup

Fixes broken xrefs, purges and audits files, and brings consultant backgrounds onto your layer and pen standards.

Sheet setup and plotting

Sets up layouts, viewports, sheet indexes and PDF plot sets ready for review or submission.
