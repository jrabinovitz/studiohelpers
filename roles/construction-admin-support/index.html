Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote Construction Admin Support Handles for Your Firm

RFI logging and routing

Logs incoming RFIs, routes them to the right architect or consultant, tracks due dates and sends reminders before responses are late.

Submittal tracking

Maintains the submittal log, checks packages for completeness against the spec section, and routes them for review.

OAC meeting minutes

Takes and distributes owner-architect-contractor meeting minutes and keeps the action item list current.

Pay application review support

Prepares pay applications for architect review by checking math, schedule of values and backup documentation.

Change order and ASI tracking

Logs ASIs, CCDs and change orders, and keeps a running record of cost and schedule impacts.

Site observation report formatting

Turns field notes and photos into formatted site observation reports for distribution.

Closeout documentation

Collects warranties, O&M manuals, attic stock records and punch list sign-offs to close out the project.
