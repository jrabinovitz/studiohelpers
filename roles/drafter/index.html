Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote Drafter Handles for Your Firm

Construction document sets

Drafts floor plans, elevations, sections and enlarged plans for permit and CD sets using your sheet templates and title blocks.

Redline pickup

Turns marked-up PDFs and hand sketches into clean, revised sheets, and keeps revision clouds, deltas and issue logs current.

BIM modeling

Builds and maintains Revit models with walls, floors, roofs, families, view templates and schedules set up to your standards.

Family and detail creation

Builds parametric Revit families and maintains standard wall, roof, window and waterproofing details so your team stops redrawing the same conditions.

Clash detection and coordination

Links consultant models, runs clash checks in Navisworks, and logs issues for your team to resolve with MEP and structural.

As-builts and existing conditions

Converts field measurements, scans and old PDFs into accurate existing-conditions drawings or Revit models for renovation projects.

Sheet setup and plotting

Sets up layouts, viewports, sheet indexes and PDF plot sets ready for review or submission.
