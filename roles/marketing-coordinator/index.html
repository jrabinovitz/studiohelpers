Day-to-Day Work

What a Remote Marketing Coordinator Handles for Your Firm

RFQ and RFP responses

Assembles proposal and qualifications packages, tracks submission requirements and deadlines, and formats them to your brand.

SF330 preparation

Builds and maintains SF330 Part I and Part II forms, resumes and project sections for federal and public-sector pursuits.

Project sheets and case studies

Writes and designs one-page project sheets with photos, scope and key facts for proposals and the website.

Resume and boilerplate library

Keeps staff resumes, firm boilerplate and project descriptions current so proposals start from a clean base.

Website and portfolio updates

Adds new projects, photography and news to your website and keeps the portfolio current.

Social media and newsletters

Schedules LinkedIn and Instagram posts and drafts email newsletters featuring your work.

Award submissions and pursuit tracking

Tracks open RFQs, award deadlines and go/no-go decisions in a pursuit log.
